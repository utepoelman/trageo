How to Play

Objective

Move your shape from your starting corner to the diagonal opposite corner. The first player to land exactly on the target square wins the round.

Scoring

  • Round Winner: Scores points based on how far the loser is from their target.
  • Calculation: Points = (Loser's Distance X + Loser's Distance Y). This is the "City Block" distance.
  • Maximum: 20 points per round.

Movement Cards

  • SLIDE: Move 1, 2, or 3 spaces. Blue cards move vertically in the direction of the arrow an the shape; Red cards move horizontally in the direction of the arrow on the shape.
  • TURN: Rotate your shape 90° clockwise or anti-clockwise.
  • FLIP: Reflect your shape. Blue cards flip your shape horizontally in the direction of the arrow on the shape. Red cards flip your shape vertically in the direction of the arrow on the shape.
  • FLIP & SLIDE: Flip your shape, then slide it.

Rules

  • Collision: If you land on the opponent's shape, they are sent back to their start (unless they are in the Safe Zone).
  • Safe Zone: The grey centre square is safe. You cannot be sent back while standing there. You cannot go there when it is occupied.
  • Confirming Moves: Click a card to preview. Click the card again (or the board) to confirm.
  • Stuck? Use the "Replace All" button to skip your turn and get a new hand.
